# 💫 About Me:
I’m a 17-year-old web developer with nearly six years of hands-on experience. I specialize in modern JavaScript ecosystems (React, Vue, Next.js, Astro) and enjoy building performant, well-structured, and maintainable applications.

I’m deeply driven by curiosity and experimentation. I explore languages that challenge my thinking—like Rust for its safety and performance model, and Lua for its simplicity and flexibility.

More than just making things work, I focus on understanding how systems function under the hood. I care about architecture, scalability, and intentional design. For me, technology isn’t a hobby—it’s a long-term craft.
